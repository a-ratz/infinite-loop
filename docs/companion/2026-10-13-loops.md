# Who Decides Done? - Companion Notes

## Concept

Part 2 of 2 in the Loops pair within "New Software Primitives". It replaces two earlier posts,
The Loopwright and The Honest Loop, with one general piece. Part 1, Fifty-One Hours, is the field
recording these rules come from.

The claim: after every attempt a loop makes one of four moves (retry, close, escalate or
accept), and the model never accepts its own work. Acceptance needs checks that ran and
evidence that was recorded.

## The bench

One simulated agent makes the same eight attempts at the same fix every time. The reader
changes the loop around it:

- what "done" means: the agent's claim, the test suite, or the test suite plus a clean review
  round
- a budget of six attempts
- a cap of three review rounds that produce only new edge cases
- escalation to a person when the budget runs out

The presets show the named failures: Freewheel ships red, "Green is done" ships a contract bug
the tests cannot see, and the spiral never stops. Bounded closes the fourth edge case with a
reason and ends with a receipt.

The script is illustrative. The cap at three rounds and the close-with-reason rule come from
the real loop described in Fifty-One Hours (pull request #625 in the Flock repository).
